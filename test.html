About BTC Lighter

BTC Lighter ($LITE) is a fun, community-first memecoin that celebrates speed and verifiability. Inspired by the low-latency exchange ethos (like Lighter.xyz), $LITE is about quick memes, on-chain receipts, and bright community-led experiments. We keep things playful: no dev tax, fair launch, and community treasury for memes & listings.

  • Fair launch — liquidity locked for 90 days
  • Community-first: treasury funds for tweets, listings, merch
  • Playful branding mixing BTC and "lighter" motif

How to buy $LITE

You can buy $LITE on any Ethereum-compatible DEX (Uniswap / Sushi / Pancake-style forks on testnets). Quick steps below:

1
Get a wallet
Install MetaMask or WalletConnect-enabled wallet and fund with ETH.
2
Connect to a DEX
Open Uniswap, connect wallet, choose "Swap".
3
Add token contract
Paste the $LITE contract address (example: 0xL1t3Example) — then select it.
4
Swap & HODL
Set slippage to 1–3% (or higher on launch), confirm swap, and enjoy the sparks.
Pro tip: ensure you use the official contract address (announced on our socials) to avoid impersonators.
